2) ITOP LIVE — PRODUCTION
--------------------------
De Product / Bang mau / Media / Tai khoan quan ly truc tiep iTop:
- Upload nguyen thu muc V7 vao hosting cung domain sontienbao.com.
- Vi du: https://sontienbao.com/stb-v7/index.html
- Dang nhap iTop tai https://sontienbao.com/admin
- Sau do mo https://sontienbao.com/stb-v7/index.html#admin

V7 se dung chinh cookie/session iTop hien tai.
Khong co username/password thu hai trong source.

3) MODULE ITOP LIVE DA NOI
---------------------------
- Authentication / session: iTop native.
- Product search: /admin/product/datatables
- Product create: /admin/product/create
- Product edit: /admin/product/{id}/edit
- Product delete: /admin/product/{id}
- Product duplicate: /admin/product/duplicate/{id}
- Product move: /admin/product/{id}/move
- Media list: /admin/media/action
- Media upload: /admin/media/upload
- Account / password: /admin/profile
- Logout: /admin/logout
- Color products: doc truc tiep tu danh muc iTop cau hinh trong itop-config.js.
